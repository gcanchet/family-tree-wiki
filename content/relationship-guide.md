## How to Trace Your Family Connections
Since this wiki is a collection of individual notes, the best way to find out how you are related to someone is to follow the **"Up and Down"** method.
### 1. The "Trace Up" Phase
Start at your own page (or the person you are starting from) and click the link for **Parents**. Keep clicking "Up" until you find a **Common Ancestor** (a shared grandparent or great-grandparent).
### 2. The "Trace Down" Phase
Once you are on the Common Ancestor's page, look at their **Children** or **Descendants** list. Follow the links down until you find the person you were looking for.
## Using the Interactive Graph
The "Obsidian-style" graph (usually found in the corner or on a dedicated page) is your best tool for seeing how people are clustered together.
 * **Hover to Highlight:** Hover your mouse over any "node" (circle) to see exactly who that person is linked to. It will dim the rest of the tree so you can see the direct line.
 * **Click to Jump:** Clicking a circle in the graph will take you directly to that person’s profile page.
 * **Zoom and Pan:** Use your mouse wheel to zoom in on specific branches, or click and drag the background to move around the family tree.
 * **Find "Hubs":** Look for the largest circles; these are usually the ancestors with the most recorded descendants and are the best starting points for "Tracing Up."
## Relationship Cheat Sheet
If you’ve found the common ancestor, use this table to define the relationship:
| Common Ancestor | Relationship |
|---|---|
| **Parents** | You are **Siblings**. |
| **Grandparents** | You are **First Cousins**. |
| **Great-Grandparents** | You are **Second Cousins**. |
| **2nd Great-Grandparents** | You are **Third Cousins**. |
### What does "Removed" mean?
You use the term **"Removed"** when people are in different generations.
 * **Once Removed:** There is a one-generation gap (e.g., your mother's first cousin is your **First Cousin Once Removed**).
 * **Twice Removed:** There is a two-generation gap (e.g., your grandfather's first cousin is your **First Cousin Twice Removed**).
## Pro-Tips for Navigating
 * **Check the Backlinks:** At the bottom of each page, the "Backlinks" section shows every other relative who has mentioned this person.
 * **Search Shortcut:** Press Ctrl + K (or Cmd + K on Mac) to quickly search for any name in the family tree.
